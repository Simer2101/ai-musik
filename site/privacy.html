Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

AImusik (“we”) is an AI music app. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Account data: email address and display name.
  • Prompts you submit to generate music.
  • Generated audio files, cover images, likes, and reports.
  • Basic technical logs needed to keep the service running.

How we use it

We use this data to create tracks, show the in-app catalog, enforce daily generation limits, moderate abuse, and operate the app. Music is generated by a third-party provider (ElevenLabs). Prompts and the resulting audio are sent to that provider solely to fulfill your request.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Public tracks and their prompts can be heard by other AImusik users. Processors include Supabase (database, auth, storage), Railway (API hosting), and ElevenLabs (music generation).

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account from the Profile screen. Deletion removes your profile, tracks, likes, and stored audio.

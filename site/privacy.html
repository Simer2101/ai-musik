Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Sonari (“we”) is an AI music app. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Data we collect

  • Account data: email address and display name.
  • Likes and reports.
  • Basic technical logs needed to keep the service running.

How we use it

We use this data to show the in-app catalog and your liked tracks, review reports, and operate the app.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Processors include Supabase (database, auth, storage) and Railway (API hosting).

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account from the Profile screen. Deletion removes your profile, likes, and reports.

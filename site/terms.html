Terms of Use

Last updated: 23 September 2026

AImusik is an application exclusively for creating and listening to AI-generated music.

What you can do

You may generate tracks from original text prompts and listen to public tracks in the catalog. Every track is labeled AI-generated.

What you cannot do

  • Ask for music that copies a specific living artist or copyrighted recording.
  • Upload or request illegal, hateful, or sexual content involving minors.
  • Attempt to extract API keys, scrape the service, or overload generation.

Rights

MVP listening is personal and non-commercial inside the app. Commercial licensing depends on the ElevenLabs plan you operate under. Do not claim human authorship or label ownership for generated tracks.

Moderation

We rate-limit generation and accept in-app reports. We may hide or delete content that violates these terms.

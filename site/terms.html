Terms of Use

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Sonari is an application for listening to AI-generated music.

What you can do

You may listen to tracks in the catalog, like them and report them. Every track is labeled AI-generated.

What you cannot do

  • Download and redistribute tracks outside the app.
  • Attempt to extract API keys, scrape the service, or overload it.

Rights

Listening is personal and non-commercial inside the app. Do not claim human authorship or label ownership for the tracks.

Moderation

We accept in-app reports. We may hide or delete content that violates these terms.
